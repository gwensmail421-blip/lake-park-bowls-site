Lake Park Bowls

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@asylummke.com. Please tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Pull back on your bowl to aim and set the weight, then let go. The flag shows where you're aiming and the curved line shows how the bowl will bend. Open How to play or Ken's walkthrough from the start menu for a guided tour.

What does Lake Park Pro include?

A one-time purchase that removes the ads between ends, unlocks every bowl colour and emblem, adds four more greens (Oceanside, Jungle, Desert and City park), and adds golden hour and floodlit night games. There is no subscription.

I bought Pro on another phone or reinstalled the game

Open the start menu, tap Lake Park Pro, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, the start menu shows a Privacy and ads link that reopens the ad consent form. On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Ken is too chatty (or too quiet)

Under Look & sound in the start menu, set Ken's voice and bubbles to Both, Voice, Bubbles or Off.
